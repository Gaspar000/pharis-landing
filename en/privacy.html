1. Identity and address of the controller

Pharis is a platform operated by Gaspar Martín, an individual with business activity (hereinafter "Pharis"), with address at Colima 134, colonia Jacarandas, 54050, Tlalnepantla de Baz, State of Mexico, Mexico.

Pharis has a personal data department that handles any matter related to this notice at privacidad@pharis.mx.

2. In what capacity Pharis processes your data

  • As your institution’s processor. When an educational institution subscribes to Pharis, the institution is the controller of its students’, instructors’ and staff’s data, and Pharis processes it on the institution’s behalf, only to provide the service and according to its instructions. In that case your institution’s privacy notice also applies.
  • As controller. Pharis is the controller of the data of people who request a demo on this site, of site visitors, and of people who use Pharis without a subscribing institution.

This notice describes all of those uses so you know what happens to your data in either case.

3. Personal data processed

Students:

  • Identification and contact: name, institutional email, institution and, if your institution’s learning platform (LMS) shows it to your instructor, phone number.
  • Academic data read from the LMS: courses, assignments and instructions, submission status, grades, last access and course material. Pharis only reads; it never changes anything in the LMS.
  • Conversations with the tutor: your questions, the answers and the topics you have doubts about.
  • Screen context: in the desktop app, a screenshot when your question needs it; in the Chrome extension, also the visible text, address and title of the open page. They are used to answer and are not stored.
  • Follow-up: if your institution uses Pharis’ channels, messages with staff, referrals to other areas and appointments.
  • Usage: how many questions you ask, when, and how long your session lasts.

Instructors and institution staff: name, institutional email, role, groups and courses, the follow-up notes and actions they record and, if an instructor connects it, their Google Calendar.

People who request a demo: name, institutional email, institution, role, approximate size of the institution and the learning platform it uses.

Site visitors: the IP address and technical data your browser sends when loading the page.

Sensitive data. If your institution handles a case through its Wellbeing area, the messages and notes of that case may include information about your health or wellbeing, which the law treats as sensitive. Those cases are marked confidential and only your institution’s Wellbeing and leadership staff can see them.

Financial data. If your institution decides to, it can share financial signals, such as an outstanding balance or a scholarship. Finance and leadership staff see the details; your instructor only sees that a balance or late payment was reported and on which date, with no amounts.

4. Purposes of processing

Primary purposes, needed to provide the service:

  • Answering your questions with the context of your course.
  • Showing your instructor which topics their groups have doubts about.
  • Computing a dropout risk signal so your institution’s staff can offer you support in time.
  • Detecting phrases in messaging that indicate a risk to your safety and alerting the staff who can help you.
  • Enabling follow-up between you and your institution’s staff: messages, referrals and appointments.
  • Managing accounts, providing support and protecting the security of the service.
  • Handling demo requests.

Secondary purposes, not needed for the service:

  • Producing aggregated statistics to understand what works and improve Pharis.
  • Sending people who requested a demo information about what is new in Pharis.

If you do not want your data used for secondary purposes, write to privacidad@pharis.mx. Declining does not affect the service.

5. Consent and how to withdraw it

For data Pharis processes as a processor, your institution obtains consent. For everything else, by using Pharis or submitting the demo form you accept this notice. In the app you are also asked to accept it expressly, and the version you accepted is recorded.

Sensitive and financial data are only processed with your express consent, except where the law provides otherwise.

You can withdraw your consent by writing to privacidad@pharis.mx with your name, the processing you want to stop and a way to reply to you. We will confirm within 20 business days. Withdrawal is not retroactive and, if it affects a primary purpose, Pharis will no longer be able to provide the service to you.

6. Automated processing and artificial intelligence

  • The tutor’s answers are generated by an Anthropic artificial intelligence model. It can make mistakes: it does not replace your instructor.
  • The dropout risk signal is computed automatically from academic variables (courses passed, enrolled and assessed). It does not use socioeconomic data. A person sees it and decides whether to contact you; no automated decisions are made about you, and it is never used to penalize or grade you.
  • Risk-language detection in messaging is rule-based, with no artificial intelligence.
  • Pharis does not train artificial intelligence models on your data, and Anthropic states that it does not train its own on it.
  • You can ask us to explain your risk signal and challenge it by writing to privacidad@pharis.mx.

7. Minors

Pharis is designed for upper secondary and higher education. If an institution gives minors access, the institution is responsible for obtaining their parents’ or guardians’ consent. Pharis has no public spaces and no advertising purposes.

9. Transfers of personal data

Pharis only transfers personal data to:

  • Your educational institution, which subscribes to the service and decides how to use follow-up information.
  • Competent authorities, when a law or a well-founded order requires it.

These transfers do not require your consent where the law allows it. If any of them were to require it and you disagree, you can object by writing to privacidad@pharis.mx; if you do not object, you are understood to accept it. Pharis does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

10. International transfers and disclosures

Pharis’ servers, database and providers are in the United States (Railway, western region). By using Pharis, your data is disclosed to that country, with the same protection described in this notice.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to Access your data (including your risk signal), Rectify it if inaccurate, Cancel it and Object to its processing for specific purposes.

Send your request in writing to privacidad@pharis.mx with:

  • Your name and a way to reply to you.
  • A document proving your identity or, where applicable, representation.
  • A clear description of the data and the right you want to exercise.
  • For rectification, the change you request and a supporting document.

We will reply within 20 business days at most and, if appropriate, carry it out within the following 15 business days. It is free of charge; only justified shipping or copying costs could apply. If you are a student of a subscribing institution, we will coordinate the response with it.

12. How to limit the use or disclosure of your data

  • Unsubscribe from informational messages or decline secondary purposes by writing to privacidad@pharis.mx.
  • Keep the app’s voice off (it is off by default) and do not connect Google Calendar.
  • Close anything you do not want to share before asking a question that uses the screen.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies. When it loads, fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. Pharis’ dashboards use a cookie and the browser’s local storage, needed to sign in, and the desktop app stores its settings on your computer, encrypted by the operating system. You can block cookies in your browser, although some features may stop working.

14. Retention periods

  • Students’, instructors’ and staff’s data is kept for as long as the institution uses Pharis.
  • When the institution stops using Pharis, all of its data is deleted; backups delete themselves within 30 days at most.
  • Demo request data is kept for up to 24 months from the last contact, unless your institution subscribes or you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • You can ask us to delete your data sooner by writing to privacidad@pharis.mx.

15. Security measures and breaches

Pharis applies administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data: encrypted connections, encrypted LMS access credentials, passwords stored as hashes, role-based permissions checked on every request and daily backups. Details are in Security and privacy.

If a breach significantly affects your rights, we will inform you and your institution without delay of what happened, the data affected and the measures taken.

16. Changes to this notice

We will publish any change on this page with the date of the last update. If the change is substantial, we will show it to you in the app and ask you to accept it again, and we will notify your institution.

17. Competent authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you can go to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, the competent authority under the LFPDPPP. If you already exercised your rights with Pharis and disagree with the response, or did not receive one, you can file a data protection request with it within the following 15 days.

18. Applicable law and jurisdiction

This notice is governed by the LFPDPPP and other applicable federal law. For any dispute, the parties submit to the competent courts of the State of Mexico.
